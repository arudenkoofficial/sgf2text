sgf2text

Turns an SGF Go game record into text a screen reader can read out: move by move, with coordinates and captured stones.

Result


        

      


      

        

          The game is converted in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
        

        

          The idea and the shape of the output come from
          aigo.tokyo/sgf-txt, by the Japan Go
          Association for the Visually Impaired. Source code:
          github.com/arudenkoofficial/sgf2text.
